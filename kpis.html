Blattner · Unit performance by jobsite

Unit KPIs

Every unit on the jobsite, joined across the Equipment Master and the JDE reports below — downtime from the transfer status history, monthly and yearly spend from the equipment rates, and rental commitments from the anniversary report. Each report's columns and tiles appear as soon as it is imported. Click a row for its full status history and per-report detail.

Import KPI reports

Drop one or more .xlsx reports — utilization / hour meter, maintenance / PM / work orders, or cost / rental / fuel. Each file's family is detected from its column headers, so filenames and column order don't matter. Files are parsed entirely in your browser; only the extracted per-unit values are published. Importing one family leaves the others untouched.
