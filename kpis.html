Blattner · Unit performance by jobsite

Unit KPIs

Every unit on the jobsite, joined across the Equipment Master and the KPI reports below. Utilization, PM and cost columns appear as soon as the matching report is imported — nothing else changes. Click a row for the full per-report detail.

Import KPI reports

Drop one or more .xlsx reports — utilization / hour meter, maintenance / PM / work orders, or cost / rental / fuel. Each file's family is detected from its column headers, so filenames and column order don't matter. Files are parsed entirely in your browser; only the extracted per-unit values are published. Importing one family leaves the others untouched.
